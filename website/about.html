Meet Martha Glory

From cult survivor
to courageous warrior.

Author, keynote speaker, TV host, trauma-informed facilitator, coach, life visionary, and founder of a growing movement for healing and freedom.

Her story

A life reclaimed—
and a purpose revealed.

Martha intimately understands the challenge of reclaiming identity beyond the constraints of cult culture.

She spent the first twenty-five years of her life inside a high-control environment marked by secrecy, fear, and trauma. After escaping, she believed she could leave the past behind and simply begin again. Instead, the pain she had buried began expressing itself across her emotional and physical life.

Her healing became a layered journey of reconnecting with her body, nervous system, voice, spirituality, and sense of self. She explored approaches including EMDR, neurofeedback, biofeedback, breathwork, yoga, meditation, craniosacral therapy, and other integrative practices. Her story is not a prescription—it is proof that there can be another way forward.

Today, Martha uses that lived experience, professional training, and spiritual wisdom to walk alongside others without judgment and remind them that trauma does not get the final word.

What grounds her work

Healing the whole person.

01

Lived experience

Martha does not speak from theory alone. She knows the fear, complexity, and courage involved in rebuilding a life.

02

Trauma-informed care

Safety, choice, compassion, dignity, and a person’s own pace guide the way she holds space.

03

Mind, body + spirit

Her work recognizes that healing can touch identity, nervous system, physical experience, relationships, and faith.

04

Community

Isolation deepens pain. Intentional connection can become a powerful part of reclaiming hope and purpose.

Beyond the work

Adventure, family,
nature, and gratitude.

Martha has a heart for adventure and connection. She loves exploring new cultures, savoring unfamiliar cuisines, chasing waterfalls, hiking mountain trails, and spending time with her large, vibrant family—especially her many beloved nieces and nephews.

That appetite for life is central to her message. Healing is not only about what we release. It is also about what becomes possible when we reconnect with joy, love, balance, belonging, and a renewed zest for life.
