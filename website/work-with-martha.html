A trauma-informed experience

What Martha brings
into the room.

Safety without judgment

Participants are invited—not pressured—to engage. Martha creates space for honesty, dignity, choice, and different starting points.

Stories with purpose

Her personal story opens doors, but the focus remains on what becomes possible for the people listening.

Mind-body awareness

Grounding, breath, reflection, and nervous-system awareness help make the experience felt—not merely heard.

Spiritual openness

Faith can be part of the conversation without denying complexity, choice, or the harm many survivors have experienced.

Warmth and energy

Martha brings honesty, humor, compassion, and the ability to hold both difficult truth and genuine joy.

A next step

People leave with language, perspective, encouragement, and a practical invitation to continue their own journey.

Signature conversation themes

Stories that help people
see themselves differently.

Every engagement is shaped for the audience and purpose.

Common themes include rewriting the story after trauma; reclaiming identity after coercive control; how trauma can show up in the body; the importance of community; faith after spiritual harm; moving from victim to courageous warrior; and learning to feel, release, heal, and live unbound.

Martha is available for corporate, nonprofit, faith-community, wellness, survivor-support, podcast, conference, and retreat settings.
