The show vision

Where pain meets purpose
and freedom becomes reality.

Let Glory Shine walks alongside trauma survivors—Battle Angels—as they reclaim their truth, rise in healing, and awaken the divine power within.

Rooted in Martha’s story from Glory: Going All In, the show exists to dismantle shame, normalize healing, and offer spiritual and emotional tools to people ready for a new chapter.

The vibe

Real. Raw. Empowering.
Sacred.

Each episode feels like sitting down with someone who has been through the valley and made it through the fire.

Expect warmth, healing, fierce truth, love, and spiritual depth—without minimizing the reality of trauma or rushing anyone’s process.

Let Glory Shine is more than a show. It is a movement of freedom, restoration, and identity reclamation.
